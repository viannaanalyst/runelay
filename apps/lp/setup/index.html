Set up runelay

About ten minutes from zero to every agent connected. Follow the steps, or hand the whole thing to your agent.

Let your agent do itPaste this into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or any agent with a terminal.

Step by step

  1. 1

    Install the server

    On any machine with Docker: a VPS, a home server or this laptop.

    $curl -fsSL runelay.com/install | sh

    It writes ~/runelay/.env with a fresh RUNELAY_SECRET. Back it up: without it, your connections cannot be opened.

    Rather not use Docker? Download the Mac app and skip to step 3.

  2. 2

    Create the owner account

    Open the first-access link the installer prints. It works once; after that, signups close.

  3. 3

    Install the CLI on your computer

    The CLI is how your agents and your machine talk to runelay.

    $curl -fsSL runelay.com/cli | sh
  4. 4

    Sign in

    Finds your server or asks for its URL, then you approve a code in the browser. No keys to paste.

    $runelay login
  5. 5

    Bring what you already have

    MCP servers, memories, skills and instructions from Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Grok, Gemini and OpenCode.

    $runelay migrate

    Accounts already logged in arrive connected. The rest wait in the panel's reconnect queue.

  6. 6

    Plug in your agents

    Registers runelay in every agent it finds. Restart them to load the tools.

    $runelay connect
  7. 7

    Stay up to date

    When a new release is out, an update button shows up in the panel. One click, no terminal.
